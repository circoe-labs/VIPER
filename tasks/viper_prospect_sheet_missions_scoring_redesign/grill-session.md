# Reconstructed product session

This handoff was reconstructed from the product discussion of 18 November 2026 and repository/source evidence.

Key product intent:
- validate the centered prospect-sheet visual direction: clean centered surface, strong alignment, generous spacing, hierarchy led by the key indicators;
- keep two tabs, Profil and Suivi, with comparable visual reorganization;
- prioritize Score prospect, a non-numeric fiche completeness state, and a new graph-based proximity score;
- add contextual actions: right-click/accessible Contact action and a Mission button;
- Mission menu should remain compact (dropdown/mega-menu, ideally 2×2), with presets: Complète cette fiche, Réanalyse son score, Cherche ses relations, Autre;
- custom mission text must support microphone dictation;
- mission is created for the currently open prospect, not as a generic unbound mission;
- rescore should use a VIPER agent and be explainable/traceable;
- final correction: Score prospect = commercial fit/desirability only; relationship graph influence is separated into Score de proximité;
- fiche quality = completeness state, not a displayed numeric score.
