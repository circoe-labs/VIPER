# Overview

## Goal
Modernize the prospect detail experience without changing VIPER's product boundaries: VIPER remains the human validation/pilot interface, IProspect remains responsible for collection/enrichment, and IContact remains responsible for contact-content preparation.

## Scope
- Prospect editor/detail UI hierarchy in Profil and Suivi.
- Prospect-score presentation and re-evaluation entry point.
- Data-quality presentation converted from numeric score to qualitative completeness state.
- New graph-derived proximity score.
- Prospect-scoped Mission menu and custom prompt with microphone.
- Context/right-click contact affordance with accessible equivalent.

## Non-goals
- Do not redesign the whole Prospection page.
- Do not copy labels/data from the generated visual when they contradict repository contracts.
- Do not replace existing mission runtime, graph, notes, or editor architecture.
- Do not introduce automated outbound contact or bypass human validation.
- Do not merge score prospect and score proximity.
